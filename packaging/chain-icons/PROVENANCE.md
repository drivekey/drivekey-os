# Chain symbols

Source: https://github.com/spothq/cryptocurrency-icons, svg/icon/{eth,sol,bnb}.svg, retrieved 2026-09-14.

Source artwork is CC0 1.0; full license retained in LICENSE.md. Terminal conversion removes circular backgrounds and shadows, preserves symbol paths and Ethereum face opacity, and accounts for 1:2 terminal cell aspect ratio. The marks identify assets/networks only; no affiliation, endorsement or trademark ownership is claimed. BNB/BSC is displayed as unavailable, not as implemented signing support.

Build conversion: node scripts/build-terminal-icons.cjs. All runtime assets are local; no network is used by the guest.
